## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an elevated, contemporary Ethiopian culinary experience. It balances deep cultural resonance with modern cosmopolitan polish. Drawing aesthetic tension from the juxtaposition of traditional earthenware, berbere spice earthiness, and crisp metropolitan typography, the interface moves past generic food-delivery templates into curated culinary journalism.

The style sits at the intersection of **Editorial Luxury** and **Warm Tactile Minimalism**:
- **Tone:** Grounded, appetizing, cultured, and impeccably organized.
- **Visual Personality:** Cream-and-charcoal parchment backdrops, rich terracotta accents reminiscent of clay *mitad* surfaces and sun-dried chilies, accented with golden spice-ochre.
- **Emotional Signature:** Generous hospitality (*gursha* culture), slow craftsmanship, yet frictionless transactional velocity for ordering, curation, and discovery.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in Ethiopian terroir and kitchen craft:
- **Primary (`#C85A32` - Berbere Terracotta):** Used for focal calls to action, badges, active tab markers, and highlight italic accents in headlines. Provides natural warmth without aggressive digital brightness.
- **Secondary (`#D99B26` - Spice Ochre / Turmeric):** Used for secondary accents, rating stars, alert pips, and specialty badges.
- **Tertiary (`#7A2E1E` - Deep Awaze Crimson):** Serves as an anchoring contrast for deep alerts, tags, and rich interactive hover treatments.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - `Neutral Dark (#1A1715)`: Deep charcoal with an espresso undertone for typography, deep surfaces, dark mode default canvas, and solid structural containers.
  - `Neutral Light (#F9F6F0)`: Warm ivory/parchment surface for light mode backgrounds, creating a soft organic reading surface instead of sterile white.
  - `Neutral Mid-Subtle (#EAE4DA)`: Hairlines, card borders, separators, and muted chip backgrounds in light mode; paired with `#2B2623` for dark mode card outlines.

## Typography

The typographical pairing evokes the rhythm of contemporary epicurean publishing:
- **Display & Headline Hierarchy:** Rendered in **Playfair Display**. Headlines make expressive use of regular weights juxtaposed with warm italic accents on evocative culinary nouns (e.g., *urban culinary craft.*).
- **Body & Functional Text:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans** for extreme legibility, comfortable line heights, and clear tabular layout when reading ingredient notes, order breakdowns, and preparation times.
- **Labels & Monospaced Touchpoints:** Category labels, delivery time tags, and administrative headers use capitalized, generously tracked (0.14em) **label-caps** for quiet precision and architectural alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around balanced modular zones:
- **Margins & Gutters:** Mobile uses a 4-column structure with `16px` gutters and `16px` lateral margins. Tablet scales to 8 columns with `20px` gutters. Desktop operates on a 12-column architecture constrained within a `1280px` (`80rem`) outer canvas, using `24px` gutters and `32px` page padding.
- **Hero Staging:** Split-level compositional balance where the primary storytelling narrative occupies 7 columns and the featured dish portrait/metadata card commands 5 columns.
- **Product Matrix:** Catalog grids dynamically reflow from 1 column on mobile screens (`<640px`), to 2–3 columns on tablet (`640px–1024px`), and up to 6 columns for dense, browsable food trays on wide desktop viewports (`>1024px`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids loud, artificial dropshadows in favor of grounded physical layering and architectural hairlines:
- **Base Canvas:** Neutral parchment (`#F9F6F0`) in light mode; rich basalt charcoal (`#1A1715`) in dark mode.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Module Containers):** Pure container separation relies on a `1px` subtle boundary (`#EAE4DA` in light mode, `#2B2623` in dark mode) paired with an ultra-diffused, warm contact ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Floating Elements, Modals, Flyouts):** Modals, search dropdowns, and sticky floating bag indicators apply `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.12)` with a slight warm tint that prevents clinical, cold gray shadows.
- **Overlays on Photography:** Media tiles utilize subtle bottom scrim gradients (`rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.72)` to `transparent`) to anchor taglines and dietary indicators over photography cleanly.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a tailored, architectural silhouette (`roundedness: 1`):
- **Default Elements:** Buttons, cards, and input controls carry crisp `4px` (`0.25rem`) to `6px` radii. This evokes printed menu placards, bespoke stationery, and structured packaging.
- **Pills for Badging:** Quick filter pills, status dots, and live order count markers employ circular pill radii (`9999px`) to immediately signal transient, tappable metadata against rectilinear cards.
- **Photography & Food Artistry:** Hero plates and catalog preview images are cropped cleanly with `4px` top corners or full rectangular framing to honor the culinary framing without distraction.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Add to Bag", "Checkout"):** High-contrast filled button. Dark Charcoal background in light mode with crisp white typography, transitioning to Warm Terracotta (`#C85A32`) upon hover or confirmation. Height: 44px (desktop), 48px (mobile). Font: `label-md`, tracked uppercase or sentence case.
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined 1px border in `#1A1715` or `#EAE4DA` with neutral text. Hover shifts subtly to muted linen background (`#F2ECE4`).
- **Tertiary & Utility Buttons:** Text-only with an animated 1px underline or subtle opacity shift (100% to 75%).

### Search & Form Inputs
- **Global Search Bar:** Low-contrast pill or soft 4px container, thin 1px border (`#E5DFD5`), embedded magnifying glass icon, light placeholder text in `body-sm`. Focused state displays a crisp 1px Terracotta outline with no blurry halo.

### Filter Chips & Navigation Tabs
- **Category Nav:** Horizontal scrolling bar of clean uppercase filter chips (`label-caps`). Inactive: subtle text with no background. Active: solid Charcoal or Terracotta fill with inverted high-contrast text.
- **Metadata Pills:** Compact delivery badges (e.g., "30–45 min Delivery", "Telebirr / Cash on Delivery") styled with a neutral tint surface (`#EFE9DF`), dark text, and an inline status dot.

### Food & Dish Cards
- **Structure:** Vertical orientation. 1:1 or 4:3 image ratio header, followed by dish title in `headline-sm`, monospaced-style price tag (`price-num`) in the header line, 2-line truncated description in `body-sm`, and an optional flex row of micro ingredient tags (`#EAE4DA` background, 10px text).
- **Interactive Badges:** Favorite heart button sits floating top-right over the dish image inside a semi-translucent 32x32px square or circle (`rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.6)` or white with charcoal icon).
- **Action Footprint:** Full-width flat rectangular "ADD TO BAG" button anchored flush at the bottom of the card.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Cuisine & Dietary Badges:** Upper-left position on imagery. High-contrast solid Terracotta or Deep Crimson tag with white uppercase 9px text.
