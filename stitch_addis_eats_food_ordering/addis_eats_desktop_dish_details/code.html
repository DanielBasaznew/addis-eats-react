The Architecture of Authentic Kitfo

skillet

Prepared according to ceremonial Wolkite custom, our Special Kitfo honors pure lean beef tenderloin from grass-fed cattle in the Bale highlands, minced fine by hand daily at our kitchen ateliers. We fold in warmed, house-churned Niter Kibbeh infused with wild korarima (black cardamom), koseret, besobila herbs, and sun-cured Arba Minch bird’s eye chili mitmita.

Prepared order-by-order to safeguard texture and delicate fat-emulsion.

spa Artisan Gomen & Ayib
